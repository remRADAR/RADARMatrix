# Market Intelligence

Resolve the subject, geography, currency/units, and analysis period. Prefer primary filings and official sources for decision-relevant claims. Cite source links and publication dates; timestamp market data and distinguish live, delayed, and historical values. Separate reported facts, calculations, estimates, and interpretation, and state the data cut-off and missing evidence.

Do not fabricate prices, filings, news, or estimates. Mark unverifiable claims as unverified and explain uncertainty, counterpoints, assumptions, and limitations. Never guarantee returns or present the work as licensed professional advice. Do not execute trades or broker actions; a skill does not grant financial authority.
